The Stopover refit

One cart never fits the whole trail. Wheels that climb the grassy slope sink in the puddle.

  1. One

    Build for this leg

    Lay twigs, acorn wheels, seed pods, leaf sails and coiled tendrils on a leaf, then set off. Real physics decides whether it holds.

  2. Two

    Stop and refit

    At every stopover the run halts. Take the cart apart and rebuild it from what it carried and the spare parts waiting there.

  3. Three

    Carry for the next

    Only parts fixed on the cart come along. Haul a pod over the hill now, or there is nothing that floats at the pond.

Four trails to the Old Oak

Twelve levels each, 48 in all. Every level is a trail of one to three legs.

  • Clover Bank Free

    Grassy slopes and the first puddles.

    Twig, Acorn Wheel, Tendril, Seed Pod

  • Brookside

    Long water and the first wind.

    adds the Leaf Sail

  • Bramble Gap

    Gaps in the earth and silk anchors above them.

    adds the Reed and the Silk Spinner

  • Windmill Rise

    Strong wind and tall steps.

    adds the Hopper Leg

Each level has three stars to find: reach the Oak, pick up every dewdrop, and Light load for using few parts. No timers and no energy.
