Privacy Policy

Inchwright, last updated 3 November 2026

Inchwright collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: which levels are open, the stars you have earned, the dewdrops you have picked up, and how many Sketches you have left.
  • Which purchases you own (Every Trail, Field Notebook), with the App Store transaction ids, so each purchase is granted only once.

This is kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone (iOS UserDefaults). It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not synced between devices. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The Clover Bank trail is free. Every Trail and Field Notebook are one-time purchases, handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the App Store which purchases you own. You can get them back with Settings → Restore purchases in the game.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
