How to play

  1. Drag parts from the pouch onto the leaf to build a cart around Inch. Wheels, pods, sails and other parts must touch a twig or reed frame; anything marked Loose stays behind.
  2. Tap Set off and watch the cart roll. Some carts show Silk and Hop buttons for your thumbs during the run.
  3. At each stopover the run halts. Check the next leg, take the cart apart and rebuild it, then tap Onward. Only parts fixed on the cart come with you.

A failed leg can be tried again from the last stopover. Stuck? A Sketch draws a working cart for the stopover you are at, which you can still change.

Purchases

The Clover Bank trail (12 levels) is free. Every Trail opens Brookside, Bramble Gap and Windmill Rise (36 levels), and Field Notebook gives unlimited Sketches. Both are one-time purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. Bought something before, or on another iPhone with the same Apple Account? Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Progress and Sketches are saved on your iPhone only. They are not synced between devices, and they are removed if you delete the app. Purchases come back with Restore purchases.
